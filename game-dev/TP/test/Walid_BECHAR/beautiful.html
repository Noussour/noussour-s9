How to play

Knock the crowned target off its tower and down to the ground.

1

Grab

Press on the bird sitting in the slingshot.

2

Pull & aim

Drag backwards. Farther = more power. The dotted line previews the path.

3

Release

Let go to launch. You have 3 birds to topple the tower.

You win when the target touches the ground: Bravo !
